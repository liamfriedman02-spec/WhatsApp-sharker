How a lead moves through Getlinked

  1. 1

    Take in every lead

    Affiliates send leads by API, postback or form. Each lead is checked on the way in for duplicates, fake details and fraud, and gets a quality score before it's routed.

    • Lead API
    • S2S postbacks
    • Duplicate checks
    • Fraud screening
    • Quality score

    Grey dots are leads stopped at the door.

  2. 2

    Send it to the right buyer

    Route by geo, language, device, source, deposit value or quality score. Set caps per country, per day and per hour, follow each broker's working hours, and weight buyers by priority or by who converts best. When a buyer hits a cap, the next lead goes to the next buyer.

    • Routing rules
    • Caps by country, day and hour
    • Working hours
    • Priority and weights
    • Best-price routing

    In the model, the arm points at the buyer getting the next lead. Each tower is as tall as that buyer's daily cap and turns grey when it's full.

  3. 3

    Give every lead a second buyer

    A lead that doesn't convert with its first buyer moves on to the next one automatically, following your rules. Sell one lead to several buyers and earn on traffic that used to go to waste.

    • Automatic redistribution
    • Multi-buyer resale
    • Rules by conversion rate or deposit value

    Light blue dots are leads that didn't convert, on their way to the next buyer.

  4. 4

    Track every deposit

    Connect brokers, brands and operators without writing code. Lead statuses and deposits sync back on their own, so you see FTDs, conversion rate and revenue per affiliate, buyer and geo as they happen. For iGaming, player reports cover GGR, deposits, bets and chargebacks.

    • No-code integrations
    • Status and deposit sync
    • Autologin
    • FTD and CR reports
    • Player reports
  5. 5

    Pay every affiliate on time

    Pay on CPA, CPL, RevShare or a hybrid deal, with different terms per affiliate, offer and geo. Balances update as deposits come in, and billing, invoices and KYC run in the same system.

    • CPA, CPL, RevShare and hybrid
    • Automated balances
    • Billing and invoices
    • KYC review

    Gold dots are deposits reported back, paying the affiliate who sent the lead.
