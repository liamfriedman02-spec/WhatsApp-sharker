How a lead moves through Getlinked

  1. 1

    Track every click

    Every affiliate gets tracking links for each offer, with deep links to any landing page. Clicks are attributed the moment they happen, by server postback or pixel, and parallel tracking keeps redirects fast.

    • Tracking links
    • Deep linking
    • Postbacks and pixels
    • Parallel tracking
    • API
  2. 2

    Send each lead to the right buyer

    Smart lead distribution rotates leads across your advertisers with the rules you set, by geo or niche. Caps and budgets enforce themselves. When a buyer hits today's cap, the next lead goes to the next buyer.

    • Lead distribution
    • Advertiser rotation
    • Capping
    • Budget management

    In the model, the arm points at the advertiser getting the next lead. Each tower is as tall as its daily cap and turns grey when it's full.

  3. 3

    See every result as it lands

    Real-time reports show clicks, leads and conversions per affiliate, offer and advertiser. Run dynamic split tests, catch suspicious traffic with fraud detection, and set alerts so problems reach you first.

    • Real-time reports
    • Dynamic split tests
    • Fraud detection
    • Alerts
  4. 4

    Pay every partner on time

    Set different commissions per affiliate and per offer. Balances update on their own as conversions come in, billing and invoices run in the same system, and KYC checks are done before the first payout.

    • Flexible commissions
    • Automated balances
    • Billing and invoices
    • KYC review

    Gold dots are payouts travelling back to the affiliate who sent the lead.
